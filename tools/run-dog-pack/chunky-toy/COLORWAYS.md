# Chonky Hounds: Doggo Dynasties — colorways

Every breed with its original look plus colorways: palettes over the breed's mask
textures (`coats.json`, `rigged/coats/`), recolored by the shader in dogcoat.js.
Colors follow the AKC/FCI breed standards; anything marked *non-standard* is a popular
or rare color the standard does not accept. Trim freely: delete a line in `SPEC`
(colorways.py) and rerun it. Edit any palette live in `colorways.html`.
