Fog math and depth

Use full opaque-ray emission and absorption on High and Medium. Draw opaque objects without their analytic fog. Integrate the existing bounded density field and actual local source radiance. Blend the resulting scatter and transmission over that opaque colour. Draw transparent sources after volume. They retain their current own-source analytic attenuation and penetration factors. This is an explicit transparent approximation. Do not claim marched absorption for every transparent source. Low and R23 off retain the legacy analytic path.

Tnext = T * exp(-deltaTau)
S += T * (1 - exp(-deltaTau)) * boundedLocalRadiance
T = Tnext
VolumeHistoryRGBA = (S, T)

Use a shared analytic-fog bypass only in the opaque stage. The independent far-city layer haze also needs this opaque bypass when that card is in the opaque role. Transparent far cards retain their own layer haze. Do not set every fog factor to zero globally. Do not add a constant ambient black floor or duplicate the old fog colour as scatter. Local light scatter uses actual sources and district tint. Any sky whisper must come from the existing sky grade and actual light response.

Residual-only scatter may be a cheap diagnostic A/B mode. It is not the default. It remains an engineering departure if selected. No operator approval of that exception is recorded.

Reconstruct each world ray from the inverse projection and camera matrix. Convert the R20 depth value to linear view depth. Convert view depth to ray distance with the ray's camera-space z component. Stop integration at the nearest opaque surface. A clear depth pixel stops at a bounded volume distance, rather than the full 14 km far plane. Start with a 2–3 km bound. Measure its visible edge before acceptance.

Use an analytic height density with denser low air and thinner high air. Add a small world-space noise variation. Use 8 steps initially at high half resolution. Permit 12 only if the measured cost remains in the gate. Medium uses 6 steps at quarter resolution. These scales refer to physical drawing-buffer width and height. Half resolution uses one quarter of the full pixel count. Quarter resolution uses one sixteenth.

Use a bounded soft distance falloff for signs and stations. A sign is a short line or rectangle light approximation. A cone source uses the actual searchlight axis and width gradient. Keep source energy finite at zero distance. Clamp invalid depth and all exponential inputs. Test finite output near light centres, camera near plane, and the sky.

Opaque depth stops rays at visible surfaces. It cannot determine whether a wall blocks the path from a light to a volume sample. R23 therefore provides unshadowed local light scatter. Existing R20 beam-depth fading remains active. Do not claim shadowed shafts through gaps or behind walls without a separate occlusion method and its measured cost.

World-space smog

Each cloud has a centre, ellipsoid size, seed, drift axis, and phase. Drift slowly along a pure periodic path from presentation time. Keep the clouds in world space. Camera-facing quads may rotate, but their centres must show parallax.

Use one instanced mesh and one material. Start with scatter-only additive clouds. This keeps instance overlap order independent and avoids a second absorption layer. Let the raymarch supply depth-aware light response. Smog supplies low-contrast shape and drift in the transparent stage. Its own-depth analytic attenuation is part of the stated transparent approximation. Do not use a bright emissive cloud sheet.
