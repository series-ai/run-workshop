Full sign fit

Require the entire sign height to lie in one face interval. Require a flat sign width to lie in the same face depth interval. Use a 4 m edge margin. For a blade, fit its mounting root in the face depth interval. Its projecting width must also keep the full blade outside |x| = 405 m. A blade may project from its slab. Its mounting root must not cross a step.

Mount flat signs at face.planeX minus the side times the existing 0.45 m standoff. Mount a blade from the same plane with the existing 0.8 m root offset. Check this relation after float32 storage. Keep a 0.05 m tolerance for the plane and rigid-frame drift. Use an explicit boundary test with a sign that fits the base tower but exceeds a narrow tier.

For ordinary signs, preserve generated size and text. Reject a candidate if no face contains it. If a small centre correction is used, compute it inside one selected face. Do not extend a face, bridge steps, or join two faces to make room.

Spacing and hero areas

Use one pure rectangle-distance function in wall coordinates. For two rectangles, dz is the positive gap between their z intervals. dy is the positive gap between their y intervals. Their edge distance is hypot(dz, dy). Use the loop wrap for neighbouring lots at the lap seam.

Require ordinary sign edge distance ≥ 2 × max(heightA, heightB). Apply this rule to flat signs, strips, outlines, and banner roots on one building façade. Compare neighbouring inner-wall lots on the same side as well. This prevents a second column of signs from bypassing the rule. Do not limit checks to identical faceId values.

Require ordinary sign edge distance from each hero composition ≥ 1.5 × the largest hero height in that composition. Reserve the union of a four-blade row as one area. Do not apply spacing between blades with the same planned composition identity. Require at least 1.5 times the largest height between separate hero compositions. Keep the four planned rows and the brand first. Move or remove standalone heroes that cannot meet this rule. Apply the exclusion to ordinary signs on nearby lots.
