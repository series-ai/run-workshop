Preserve source luminance

Apply color changes to complete emission contributions in linear RGB:

Y(c) = dot(c, [0.2126, 0.7152, 0.0722])
unit(h) = h / max(Y(h), 0.000001)
recolor(c,h,s) = Y(c) × mix(unit(neutral), unit(h), s)

This preserves source luminance. Do not clamp HDR RGB to one. Keep black sources black.

Rooms should keep low-saturation district light. Bright selected room lamps can use more color. The grid itself must remain neutral. Preserve building occupancy, brightness profiles, and room detail. Do not multiply a district tint into the existing warm pane, warm atlas, and warm glass colors.

Use high saturation for the brightest 15% of eligible accent sources. Use lower saturation for background sources. Verify this in rendered frames. A hash that selects 15% of sources does not select the brightest 15% of image pixels.

The current distance grade at city.ts:1794 changes luminance. Its steel target has luminance about 0.776. Preserve the old total brightness factor when normalizing its color:

g(k) = (1 − k + 0.776 × k) × (1 − 0.55 × k)
newGrade = mix(c, Y(c) × unit(steel), k) × g(k)

This preserves the old graded luminance while making desaturation independent of brightness.

Measurement limits

Hue is unstable near grey and black. Keep those pixels separate in hue counts. Report luminance-weighted energy separately from pixel counts. Bloom spreads one source across many pixels. Magenta and cyan combine into other hues. Tone mapping can reduce saturation even when linear luminance is preserved.

The interior atlas uses SRGBColorSpace. The far atlas uses NoColorSpace. Use the correct conversion for each. Do not use reduced occupancy or a darker background to claim a color-only improvement.

The measurement script keeps the original saturated bright-neon mask: HSV saturation ≥0.35 and max sRGB code value ≥0.45. All-pixel linear energy keeps dim colors, grey, and white. Blue has a separate family. The script reports full frames and the old centre crop separately.

State the energy target denominator before comparison. Whole-frame energy and saturated neon energy are different measures. Do not select a denominator after measurement to make the target pass.
