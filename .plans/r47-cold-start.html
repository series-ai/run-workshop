Method and declared limit

The requested cost limit is no repeatable Low cold-start increase. The 0 ms limit is not proven by this run. The specific +25.2 ms estimate does not survive this check. Keep those two conclusions separate.

Run 18 pairs per tier, in six blocks of three pairs. Alternate A → B and B → A on each pair. Keep both arms adjacent. Use a fresh browser context per arm. Use one browser and one page at a time. Use a 1280 × 720 viewport. Use native time. Require a boot-cache hit in every run. Suspend each scene after its first frame. No build or test job from this thread ran during the paired check.

Limit: other jobs share the machine. No quiet window was available. Five other headless browser roots were confirmed before the run. They belong to other workspaces. No other job was stopped. Adjacent arms share much of the load. Alternating order reduces bias from session drift. It does not remove all load noise. Each pair records the load average and GPU use before each arm. Each block records CPU idle before and after its three pairs.

The merged candidate also includes window commit f2af3483 and traffic commit 85f970b6. These pairs compare the selected builds. They do not assign a small residual cost to one module.

The first-frame metric records submission, not GPU completion. The bootstrap uses seed 4747. The pair interval uses 10,000 resamples of 18 deltas. The block interval uses 20,000 resamples of six whole blocks. These intervals are estimates under the observed shared load. They are not hard latency bounds.

The second atlas costs 16 MiB before mipmaps and about 21.3 MiB with mipmaps in every tier. Low draws two holograms. All 106 screens remain. They can use all ten brands and all forty ad frames. The screen frames use most of the occupied atlas area.

A 1024 × 1024 atlas can keep the normalized addresses and all content. Its screen cells would change from 512 × 128 to 256 × 64. Its hologram cells would change from 256 × 256 to 128 × 128. Its base memory would fall from 16 MiB to 4 MiB. That is a possible future quality trade. It requires a visual comparison. This measurement does not prove that Low never shows the extra detail.

No render source changed in this check. High, Medium, and Low keep their media content and resolution. Screens keep all cuts. Holograms keep their counts. No content was removed to improve a timing number.
