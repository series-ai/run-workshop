# Promo: script (draft)

Built for RUN: the trailer ends on the RUN logo. Portrait 1080 × 1920 (decided), 30 fps, about 51 s, cut to 112 BPM (one bar ≈ 2.14 s; every shot is a whole number of bars).
Same approach as the Critter Cubes promo (`tools/run-dice-animals/promo/`): everything on screen is the real kit
running in a small three.js "pet sim" scene, so it reads as gameplay capture. Text, captions and a light care-game HUD
(a water drop, a heart, a sun) are drawn into the frame.

**Tone:** cosy and playful: a bit toddler, a bit puppy. Warm window light, a rug, a couch, a sunny sill.
**Music:** a bouncy, plucky track at 112 BPM: ukulele, marimba, soft kick and shaker, a whistle hook in the chorus.
**SFX:** pitter-patter feet, soft clay *tok*s when pots land, leaf rustles on every wobble, a pop when limbs come out,
a squeak on the slide, water sploshes on the shake.
**Logo:** `promo/logo-terra-tots.png` (chosen: walking terracotta letters with sage-green feet, a green sprouting pot as
the o in Tots). Needs redrawing as a clean vector before the final render. Captions use the same soft rounded lettering. Letters pop in one at a time; the captions bounce like a hop.

## Name

Working title: **Terra Tots**. Terracotta pot bodies with toddler energy; short, cute and easy to search.

| Option | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Terra Tots** (recommended) | terracotta + tots; says "pot" and "little kid" at once |
| Potlings | pot + -lings (sprouts, little ones); sounds like a creature name |
| Pot Pals | instantly clear, very friendly; more generic |
| Sproutlings | leans on the growing plants rather than the pots |
| Pot to Trot | the game's working title; a fun pun, but reads as a game more than a kit |
| Leaf Me Alone | jokey; good for a tagline, weak as a pack name |

**Tagline options:** *Little pots with big personalities.* · *Grow them. Dress them. Watch them waddle.* · *Houseplants
that need a hug.*

## What it shows off (checklist)

- Plants grow leaf by leaf (rolled → baby → mature), with real-species leaf shapes
- Rare variegated cultivars as a two-colour pattern layer
- Pots: 36 shapes, colourways, two-colour patterns
- Bodies fit any pot; arm and leg lengths change
- Skin markings, mix and match per limb, colourways
- 26 animations with personality: walks, dances, actions, couch moments
- Secondary motion: leaves follow through on every move, soil stays put
- Props: couch, seed pod, sweat and water drops
