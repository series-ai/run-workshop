# Terra Tots trailer (outline, v3: the cast)

Portrait 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, about 83 s, built for RUN and ending on the RUN logo. Everything on screen is the real kit
in three.js, in the toon look with cross-lighting. Earlier versions of this outline are superseded.

**Rules (unchanged)**
- **Only show what's in the kit.** A plain floor and backdrop. The only props are the watering can and the bug spray.
- **Keep text minimal.** The text is the intro cards, the chalk lesson, the bug counts, the title and the tagline.
- **No named setting.** It's a day in a sunny room full of tots, told through the bits.
- **The camera** is high and tipped down; crowds fill the frame and run off its edges. The music never cuts out.

## The cast

Ten characters. Each gets an **intro card** the first time they do their signature bit: a lower-third pop-up with
their name in the logo lettering, their plant in small type, and a quippy card line (their archetype, then the joke), on screen for about a
bar while they keep animating.

## To build

- **The intro card:** a lower-third panel (cream pill or paper tag) with the name in the logo lettering, the plant in
  small caps, and the personality line. It pops in on the beat and slides out after a bar.
- **One variegated leaf on a plain monstera:** the plant builder needs to give a single leaf the Albo pattern. That's
  also a nice kit feature ("sports": the odd variegated leaf).
- **The pedigree add-on:** a new chalk branch and a sparkle drawn onto the slate after the new leaf.
- **Cast in every crowd:** the cast mixed into the field, plus the ten in the parade and the end card.
- **The class photo swaps:** prebuilt variants per tot (pot, plant, limbs, proportions) shown on a wave front, the
  same trick as the earlier pot line-up but one attribute per bar.
