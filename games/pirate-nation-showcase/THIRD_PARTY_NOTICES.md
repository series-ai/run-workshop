# Third-Party Notices — Pirate Nation Art Showcase

This tool bundles a large third-party art & audio pack. The pack is MIT
licensed; keep the copyright notice with any redistribution.

## Proof of Play — Pirate Nation art & audio pack

- Files: the four remote packs published in `jam-ready-assets` (375 GLB
  models, 355 generated JPEG previews, 513 PNG sprites, and 30 audio tracks),
  plus the local catalogs under `public/catalog/pirate-nation/` and
  `src/avatar/avatarCatalog.generated.ts` (generated from the same source).
- Author: Proof of Play, Inc.
- License: MIT — Copyright (c) 2026 Proof of Play, Inc. See
  `public/catalog/pirate-nation/LICENSE`.
- Source: https://github.com/proofofplay/piratenation-game at commit
  `dc921b650c8a1d7c449b2a3553949d3e7c8266e9` (archival open-source release).
  Per-file provenance: `public/catalog/pirate-nation/PROVENANCE.md` and the
  `sourceRelativePath` field of every catalog entry. Model previews are
  generated derivatives stored under the model category `Previews/` folders
  in the jam 3D pack.

The extraction is allowlisted: paid/third-party packs from the source
repository (Honeti GUI, Pixel Art Icons, Animated Loading Icons, Meebits,
DemoPlaceholder art, commercial `SND####` sound-library files) are excluded.
