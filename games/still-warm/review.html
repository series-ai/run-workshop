What changed

  • Exact contact signals let the father understand and stop the next movement.
  • STOP, blackout, pause, and old session callbacks cannot issue stale patient commands.
  • A failed model request can recover without resetting the room.
  • A request that ignores STOP is retired after three seconds. Continue reconnects to the same room state.
  • Safe paths keep the son outside the body. His return pace follows the source walk.
  • Thread, cloth, water, and candle flame have consistent state and visible results.
  • The mouth and fingers return to their authored motion after temporary changes.

Checked in the game

  • A full live operation and a full guided route reached the saved ending.
  • The latest guided route took 5½ minutes, with STOP, retry, and pause.
  • HTTP 500 recovery preserved the world. A real STOP canceled contact before it took effect.
  • The mobile route reached blackout and paused. The controls fit at 390 pixels.
  • 188 tests pass. Production and proof builds pass.

The live run needed a direct pain-relief instruction. It finished with very little blood. This is evidence from one run, not a guarantee that the son will choose the same actions again.
