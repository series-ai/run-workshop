Verification & Architecture

  • Mathematical geometry: calculateEyelidPaths maps progress non-linearly: 0–22% creates a horizontal slit (max 14px in 1000-unit box, tapered at corners); 22–100% applies a smooth quadratic ease parting the upper lid upward and lower lid downward.
  • Non-blocking animation: Driven via single-trigger requestAnimationFrame loop in Awakening.tsx with delicate flutter, unaffected by 100ms clock interval ticks.
  • Accessibility: Respects prefers-reduced-motion: reduce by bypassing animation delay and rendering target progress directly.
  • All 235 unit tests pass across 15 test suites in Vitest.
