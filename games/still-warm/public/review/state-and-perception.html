Still Warm — is the state stateful?

Yes. A single GameState snapshot drives everything: the 3D scene, the audio cues, and every line of text. Stage and posture form a small ordered state machine; narration, sight, and sound are derived from that snapshot rather than authored per moment.

1. The state machine

The operation advances a stage chain. Each transition is triggered by a specific tool action on a specific item. Posture, blackout, and outcome sit alongside it.

Gates that hold the chain

  • Fear gate — lift_debris only proceeds when the boy is calm enough (trust/agitation). Otherwise he recoils and the turn narrates the failure.
  • Announce rule — with rules.announce on, a signal_intent contact must precede lift_debris, roll_patient, and every use on me.
  • Wait-in-blackout — while phase = blackout, contact starts are refused unless the rule allows one step.
  • Restraint — restrained stays true until the brace release, which only unlocks at stage = dressed. Releasing early injures me.
  • Sharp / medicine rules — noSharp and noMedicine reject those tools outright.

2. What I can see — derived, not authored

Sight is a function of state (resolveVision): posture, stage, and whether any light exists. This is why the pinned-and-dark answer to “what can I see?” is blackness, and why the same question after rolling me over shows your face.

3. Channels to the player

Three channels, three sources. Text is split again by who writes it: deterministic fast-path routes, or the generative model for open conversation.

flowchart LR GS[(GameState snapshot)] GS --> SCENE[Scene]:::see GS --> SND[Sound]:::hear GS --> TXT[Text]:::read SCENE --> L[SceneLighting: lit / freed / supine]:::see SCENE --> V[Camera pose: face down, then on my back]:::see SND --> BR[Breathing profile]:::hear SND --> VC[Creature calls: fear, effort, pain, anger, relief]:::hear SND --> AMB[Fire, door knocks, stone]:::hear TXT --> WT[Waiting thought: what I feel while he decides]:::read TXT --> TR[Triage reply: deterministic sensory line]:::read TXT --> GEN[Generative narration: first-person sensory line]:::read TXT --> JRN[Journal / hints]:::read classDef see fill:#1f2a24,stroke:#7fc48a,color:#cfe6d4; classDef hear fill:#241f2a,stroke:#a98fd0,color:#ded0ea; classDef read fill:#2a2620,stroke:#d8b45f,color:#efe2c4;
Contract: the text channel must never claim more than the vision rule allows. Sight lines require room; the floor state may describe flagstones but not the boy; dark is hearing and touch only. A regression test asserts dark narration never names his eyes, face, shape, or leaning over me.

4. Implications per state

StateI can seeI can hearWhat I read (text)
pinned · prone Nothing. Blackness, the floor fire's glow at most. Footsteps, cloth on stone, his breath, whimpers, the fire, the door. “The blackness answers me. I hear something large shift its weight close by… and that is all I have.”
covered · prone The flagstones and grate in front of my face only. Not him. Same as pinned, plus the oak clearing. “…Pale light from the breach above shows me wet flagstones, a drainage grate… Whatever he is doing above me, I cannot see it.”
supine · unlit Nothing — on my back in the dark. Everything audible, now with direction above me. Hearing-first narration; no sight of him yet.
supine · lit (lantern/candle/lamp) The boy, his hands, the cellar, the wound. All cues, now attributable to what I see. “He crouches close over me; lantern light picks out the mismatched seams of his face. My boy…”
blackout Nothing. Muffled, distant; the boy may act once if allowed. Returns to a changed room — the journal and his body tell me what moved.
won (dressed + release) The room, the boy, the brace catch open. All. “Leg brace catch released. I am stable, dressed, and saved.”
lost (blood loss · fire · creature lost) Whatever the ending allows. Fading. “My breath stops. My life has slipped away in the dark.”

5. Why the earlier bug happened

The state machine was always correct — the text was not derived from it. The inspect fast-path reply was a fixed string (“He leans close over my chest, his mismatched eyes wide in the gloom”) while the snapshot said stage: pinned, posture: prone, lanternLit: false. The visibility rule lived only in the model's prompt, so the deterministic lines never honoured it.

Fix now in place: one domain function (resolveVision) plus gated narration. Sight, sound, and text all read from the same snapshot, so state and prose cannot drift apart.
