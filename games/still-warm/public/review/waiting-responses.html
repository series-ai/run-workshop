Buffering Pauses & Pacing

The typewriter effect now features dedicated trailing pauses to act as realistic buffers while waiting for the model:

  • Line 1 Trailing Buffer: Ends with ...{{pause(2.2)}}. Types out the first sensory thought, then pauses at the dots for 2.2 seconds before advancing.
  • Line 2 Trailing Buffer: Ends with .{{pause(2.5)}}. If the response has not yet arrived, types the second thought and buffers for an additional 2.5 seconds at the period.
  • Total Buffer Window: ~7.2 seconds of atmospheric sensory immersion, seamlessly replaced when the creature's response arrives.

State-Dependent Thought Filtering

Thoughts are filtered based on the player's physical reality:

  • Prone Only (Face-Down): "The damp stone bites into your forehead.", "The cellar floor drains the warmth from your skin.", "The cold stone muffles your call". These are filtered out once the patient is rolled over.
  • Pinned Only: "Your ribs groan beneath the oak cabinet." Only available before the debris is lifted.
  • Supine Only (After Being Flipped Over): "The damp cellar ceiling looms in the shadows above.", "You stare upward into the black rafters.", "Your exposed wound burns in the raw air."
  • Always Available: General vocal friction ("Your throat is coarse", "The words scrape against your dry lips", "Your voice echoes in the darkness") and chest sensations.

Model Optimization & Local Logging

  • Quick Model Class: modelClass: "quick" with fallback list ["gpt-5.4-mini", "gpt-5.6-luna"] configured on Venus transport for minimal turnaround latency.
  • Guaranteed Non-Verbal Creature Answer: In instructions.ts, "silence is valid" has been removed; the creature must always respond to the player's voice. live.ensureResponse() guarantees an emotional response even if the model turn didn't invoke interpret_response.
  • Local Conversation Logging: All player commands, tool executions (inspect_room, act, interpret_response), observations, errors, and responses are appended to conversation.log via the Vite dev server endpoint /_log_conversation, stored in window.__STILL_WARM_CONVERSATION__, and logged to DevTools console.
