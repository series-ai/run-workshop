Feature Verification

Procedural Waiting Thoughts & Non-Verbal Creature

When the player submits a command, the game now provides a gothic waiting state with procedural sensory thoughts drawn from over 200 possible combinations. The typewriter effect uses pauses at the ellipsis ... to slow down before revealing a second non-repeating thought if the response has not yet arrived. Furthermore, the creature/boy never speaks back with words—responses are strictly non-verbal emotional reactions (moans, slurred speech, excitement, screaming, or primal vocalizations) synchronized with synthesized Web Audio vocal cues.

Combinatorial Thought Library (>200 Options)

Built from 15 Set 1 sensory vocal phrases and 15 Set 2 bodily/cellar continuation phrases. Keyword collision filtering ensures zero semantic repetition (e.g. avoiding repeating "throat" or "stone").

Creature Non-Verbal Emotional Repertoire

The creature is incapable of human speech. Responses are categorized by vocal emotions and mapped to synthesis parameters:

  • Moan "A low, shuddering moan in the dark." / "A heavy, guttural moan from across the cellar floor."
  • Slurred Speech "A thick, slurred murmur—he tries to form a word, but only a clumsy sound spills out."
  • Excitement "A sharp, eager rasp—he shifts his weight in sudden excitement."
  • Screaming "A sudden, terrified scream echoes off the cellar walls."
  • Fear / Whimper "A frightened, shuddering whimper close to the stone."
  • Effort "A deep, straining grunt under the heavy weight."
  • Relief "A long, shuddering sigh of relief."
  • Anger "A low, defensive rumble vibrating in his throat."
