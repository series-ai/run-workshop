10 Refinement Cycles Completed

In accordance with the /loop until Astra says you've attained the concept art visual target directive, the real-time WebGL2 engine was iteratively submitted to GPT-6 Astra (via the rundot CLI) with in-engine captures compared directly against the Concept 03 reference. Below is the systematic audit of critiques and resulting architectural enhancements:

Rounds 1–4: Tone & Lighting Foundations

Astra Critique: Pitch-black crushed shadow cavities, single brown ambient light, missing overhead structural crane and demolition equipment.

Engine Solution: Added initial gantry crane framing, increased directional light intensity to 1.55, and introduced warm tungsten yard floodlights.

Rounds 5–6: Shader & Geometry De-Noising

Astra Critique: Per-voxel DDA procedural chamfer created noisy tessellation lines on vehicles; ACES tone mapping crushed shadow values < 0.05 to pure black.

Engine Solution: Removed voxel bevel/AO lines from VoxelVolumeRenderer.tsx; switched tone mapping to THREE.NeutralToneMapping (exposure 1.15) for linear shadow preservation.

Rounds 7–8: Sky Separation & Focal Action

Astra Critique: Large black rectangular trench grates broke ground plane; red sky band washed upper zenith; gantry beam cropped by upper viewport.

Engine Solution: Removed 32m trench grates; rebuilt SkyDome.tsx with 3-tier elevation curve (blue-charcoal #15232D zenith); lowered crane to 8.8m with safety orange #DE681F center webs.

Rounds 9–10: Salvage Hierarchy & Car Silhouettes

Astra Critique: Floor puddles read as dark voids; scene needed recognizable crushed cars with cabins and wheels; hero cutting rig required bloom simulation.

Engine Solution: Removed dark puddle planes; added 3 tiered crushed vehicles (faded teal #517D78, oxidized red #945546, dirty mustard #C99B31) with wheels and folded roofs; added 180 cascading sparks + additive glow halo card.
