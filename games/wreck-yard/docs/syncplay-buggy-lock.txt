Wreck Yard -> Syncplay: rc.39 vehicle lock on a conveyor support

Observed in the game
- Driving near the car under the conveyor can stop the buggy.
- In our reproduction, the locked chassis contacts the conveyor support
  and the floor. It does not contact the car.
- Reverse input reaches the SDK. The vehicle remains active.

Verified against the published 6.0.0-rc.39 SDK
- The isolated world contains only a floor, a conveyor post, and the original
  compound chassis. No game tick, input, damage, or grab code runs.
- Settle for 60 ticks. Drive forward for 90 ticks. Reverse for 120 ticks.
- Reverse distance: 0.001754 m in 4 seconds.
- Rear wheels have ground contact and tire forces remain nonzero for all
  120 reverse ticks. This is not a parked-input or all-airborne state.
- A box chassis with the same mass, inertia, and vehicle definition reverses
  43.177 m in the same setup.
- The isolated car-only sweep reverses in all 24 cases. Removing the car
  from the conveyor test gives the same locked result.

Confirmed contact-generation fault
- Conveyor post: box half extents (0.14, 3.1, 0.14), center (-0.5, 2.24, 2.7).
- Chassis child 0: front bumper box half extents (0.56, 0.08, 0.04),
  local offset (0, -0.56, -1.48).
- At a locked pose, a native overlap of that child and the post returns
  a contact point around world (0.157452, 3.219724, 2.807623).
- In chassis local space the point is (0.044983, 0.918186, -2.959162).
  This is outside the bumper bounds by 1.398186 m vertically and 1.439162 m
  along z. It is also outside the complete chassis voxel bounds.
- The primitive box overlap already returns the invalid point. The compound
  merge and event formatter are not its source.

Source path in the rc.39 tarball
core/native/physics/physics.cpp:
- 730-736: boxSupport chooses the positive corner for an axis with zero dot.
  The post contact normal has y=0, so the post support point uses its top.
- 1037-1047: boxBoxOriented takes the midpoint of two support corners.
- 822-825: populateBoxContactPoints retains that midpoint for an edge case.
- 5237-5245 and 7136-7142: the contact response uses that point for its levers.
The fixture uses the default NativeWorldPose path. It does not use the
alternate material witness route. The invalid point gives a large false
lever arm. The movement result after a native fix remains to be checked.
We have not changed or rebuilt the SDK kernel.

Requested fix
Compute contacts on the two finite support edges for edge-edge box contacts.
Use their closest witness points. Keep contacts near both shapes within
contact tolerance. Cover rotated box children against tall, narrow boxes.
Do not clear vehicle velocity or remove prop collision to fix this case.

Release gates
1. The native reproduction reverses more than 1 m after the impact.
2. All post contact points stay inside the expanded chassis bounds. Add an
   exact child-bound gate for the front bumper primitive fixture.
3. Repeat with a yaw offset, side impact, static post, and dynamic car.
4. Rollback and late join during the contact remain bit-identical.
5. Existing ramp, airborne momentum, light-scrap, braking, and parking gates pass.
