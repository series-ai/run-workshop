Camera update and frame budget
Checked 2026-10-11 with Syncplay 6.0.0-rc.40.

Camera look uses local input on each render frame. It does not wait for a
simulation tick. Syncplay receives quantized aim at 30 ticks per second.
Walking uses interpolated player positions from runner.getRenderState().

The browser check stops simulation time and moves the view. Both mouse drag
and touch drag still turn the camera. Walking moves the camera between ticks.
The final desktop and mobile-layout samples ran near 60 FPS on this Mac.

Two graphics faults were fixed:
1. The resolution policy accepted about 30 FPS before it reduced resolution.
   Its default budget is now 1000 / 60 ms.
2. Canvas restored its initial resolution after parent renders. YardScene
   now owns the resolution value. Both Canvas and DynamicDpr use that value.

Under a fixed CPU load, the old policy ran at about 48.5 FPS without reducing
resolution. The new policy reduced mobile DPR from 1 to 0.85 in about 0.56 s.
That value stayed in effect through tool changes and recovery. The test adds
CPU load to one scene render. It does not measure a weak phone GPU.

The policy retains an 8 ms reserve before it increases resolution. A steady
60 Hz display therefore retains a reduced resolution for the scene session.
The render loop has no 60 FPS cap. It can update on higher refresh displays.
